without violating, with a faithful observer and unmeasured-load rejection.

neural actor-critic on real-sim rollouts; env-free launch; no gray-box plant
as the controller; no classical PID/LQR/MPC as the product; imagination actor
stays deleted. One GPU job at a time.

STANDING RESIDUALS — never retire because a knob family closed.
MV oscillation is ALLOWED. Fail/optimize on CV smoothness + limit hugging + TM + unmeasured DR.

  R1 TM: MV ss 0.927 / DV ss 0.893; compounding 1step→OL 0.85; score curve_iae_normed (shape) not ss-ratio alone. P26 MV ss 0.973 remains the gain lock.

  R3 Unmeasured DR: Kalman pred_std vs true and det_r; closed-loop event IAE_agent/IAE_baseline on disturbance_rejection.png.

  * CV total variation as a smoothness gate (slow ride to the limit is good)

  * VALID 9/9, GAIN-READY, all_pass, or family-closed as "residual closed"
  * requiring a beat of P64 paired-econ to KEEP a CV-smoothness, headroom, or DR win
After EXIT, write which metrics you trusted, which you put on trial, and why.

FREEDOM TO REFACTOR — you are not limited to one-knob N+1 on an open family.
Default when a family is still open AND the metric is causal for the goal:
  largest residual, one attributed change.
You MAY and SHOULD do a bigger refactor (observer architecture, OP-aware
gains, Kalman form, actor-critic training signal, reward/objective, freeze
and quality gates, metric definitions) when: the family is closed; N+1 would
be lottery (extra-P1, leftover DREAMER_*, stacking critic knobs); train
metrics disagree with val plots; or improving the current score cannot put a
smooth CV on the economic limit. Stay inside the product envelope above.
Changing piece (observer / Kalman / actor-critic) OR taking the same residual
to another plant then RETURNING to test_sim remains valid; a refactor on the
current plant is also valid.
