## Envelope (bounds, not the recipe)
Four pieces, all in service of a neural controller. You may change internals, replace a piece with a neural successor, or refactor the wiring. You may not ship a linear/gray-box plant, a classical PID/LQR/MPC, or a DV-only feedforward hack as the product.
1. Observer — learned state-space / sequence model (RSSM, TSSM, or successor: encode, roll dynamics, decode CVs). Recover MV→CV and DV→CV gains and dynamics, not just next-step correlation. MV and DV are both measured inputs; do not special-case DV.
2. Neural Kalman / disturbance observer — estimates the unmeasured load. Other neural Kalman forms are allowed. Classical Kalman on an identified linear plant is not. Do not drop the disturbance estimator and hope reconstruction absorbs the load unless you have evidence that it is preferable over the Neural Kalman architecture.
3. Actor-critic — policy + value on observer (and disturbance) features, acting on the real plant. Training method is open (real rollouts, imagination, hybrids, critic/advantage/entropy). The deployed controller is the neural policy, not an expert or optimizer in the loop. Expert BC is a launchpad, not a ceiling. A collapsed critic is an open problem, not a taboo.
4. Simulators — plants that should mimic chemical processes. test_sim is the sandbox. Other bundled sims are more complex/nonlinear. If the workflow stalls after a fair VALID test_sim run, audit the simulators (units, excitation, hidden load, data) before adding knobs. New sims only if needed; at most 10. Stay on test_sim until at least one VALID run beats baseline with a healthy observer (actor_experiment_valid). Then other plants are in scope.
Current defaults (real-sim actor, frozen observer at P3, RSSM, DOB on) are a starting point. Change them when evidence says so.
Keep the stack fully neural through ONNX: one graph (obs window, prev actions) → action.
